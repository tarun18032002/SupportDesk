# ShopLite - Cancellations and Address Changes
Policy version: demo-v1. Fictional policies for an educational demo, not a real store.

An order may be canceled from the Orders section of the customer's account within 60 minutes after placement, provided warehouse processing has not begun. Both conditions must hold. A request made 90 minutes after placement is outside the self-service cancellation window, even if the order has not yet shipped.

The delivery address can be changed during that same 60-minute window only while warehouse processing has not begun. After the window closes or warehouse processing begins, support may review a request, but cancellation or an address change is not guaranteed.

Customers should not create a second order on the assumption that the first has been canceled. If cancellation is not possible, the delivered product may be eligible under the Returns Policy. Eligibility depends on the product's condition and the time since delivery.

The assistant cannot access order status, cancel an order, change an address, or confirm that processing has begun. It can only explain these rules.
