# ShopLite - Shipping Policy
Policy version: demo-v1. Fictional policies for an educational demo, not a real store.

ShopLite ships only within the contiguous United States. International shipping and delivery to Alaska or Hawaii are not available in this demo policy.

Standard shipping costs USD 5 for a merchandise subtotal below USD 50. Standard shipping is free when the merchandise subtotal is at least USD 50 after discounts and before tax. Express shipping costs USD 12 regardless of order value. Express shipping is not included in the free-standard-shipping offer.

Orders normally take 1 to 2 business days to process before dispatch. After dispatch, standard delivery normally takes 3 to 5 business days, while express delivery normally takes 1 to 2 business days. These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

A tracking link is emailed after dispatch. The assistant has no access to live tracking or order records and cannot locate an individual package, even when given an order number. Customers should use their tracking link or contact support.
