# ShopLite - Account Help and Support
Policy version: demo-v1. Fictional policies for an educational demo, not a real store.

To reset a forgotten password, select Forgot password on the ShopLite sign-in screen and enter the email associated with the account. Use the link in the reset email. If the message does not appear, check spam or junk folders. The assistant cannot generate reset links or verify that a reset email was sent.

Support is available through the Help section in the customer's account, Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 America/New_York time, excluding public holidays. The expected first response is within 2 business days; this is not a guarantee of resolution.

A customer may provide an order number to the real support team for order-related inquiries. The demo assistant has no access to customer accounts, identity records, tickets, or order databases. It cannot change an email address, reset a password, create a support ticket, or take actions on a customer's behalf.

Do not request passwords, full card details, security codes, or one-time authentication codes. No real customer data should be added to this demonstration dataset.
