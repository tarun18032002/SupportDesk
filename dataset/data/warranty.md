# ShopLite - Product Warranty
Policy version: demo-v1. Fictional policies for an educational demo, not a real store.

ShopLite electronic products include a limited 12-month warranty beginning on the delivery date. It covers manufacturing defects that appear during normal use. Proof of purchase is required. An electronic product that develops a manufacturing defect 6 months after delivery may qualify if the other conditions are met.

The warranty excludes accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse, unauthorized repairs, and ordinary cosmetic wear. It does not extend the 30-day change-of-mind return window. Arrival damage should instead be reported under the separate Damaged Items Policy.

Submit a warranty request through the account's Help section with the order number, a description of the defect, and the product serial number. After assessment and approval, ShopLite may repair or replace the product. Do not promise an automatic cash refund or guarantee claim approval.

The assistant cannot inspect a product, determine the cause of a fault, look up warranty registration, or approve a warranty claim.
