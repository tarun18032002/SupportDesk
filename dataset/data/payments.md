# ShopLite - Payments and Discounts
Policy version: demo-v1. Fictional policies for an educational demo, not a real store.

ShopLite accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. It does not offer cash on delivery, cryptocurrency payments, or installment plans under this demo policy. All displayed amounts in this knowledge base are in US dollars.

Only one promotional discount code may be used per order. Discount codes cannot be combined with each other. A code must be entered at checkout before payment; this policy does not promise that a code can be applied retroactively. Product-specific exclusions may be shown with the promotion.

A payment failure does not necessarily mean the customer's bank account lacks funds. Customers should check their payment details or contact their payment provider. The assistant cannot see declined-payment reasons, payment credentials, available balances, or bank transactions.

No dates or discount amounts for future sales are published in these documents. The assistant must not invent upcoming promotions. Never ask a customer for a full card number, security code, password, or one-time authentication code.
