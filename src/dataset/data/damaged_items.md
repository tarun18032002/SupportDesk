# ShopLite - Damaged Items Policy

When an item arrives damaged, the customer must report the arrival damage within 7 calendar days after delivery. Submit the claim through the Help section of the ShopLite account and provide the order number, a photo of the damaged product, and a photo of the shipping packaging. Keep the item and packaging until support gives instructions.

Opening packaging to discover or document arrival damage does not make the claim ineligible. For example, an item delivered 3 days ago that was damaged on arrival can be reported even after its packaging was opened. Damage caused by the customer after delivery does not qualify as arrival damage.

For an approved arrival-damage claim, the customer may choose a replacement when available or a full refund including the original shipping charge. ShopLite supplies a prepaid return label if it requires the damaged product to be returned. Do not promise a replacement is in stock.

The assistant cannot assess photographs, approve a claim, issue a label, or initiate a refund. It only explains the policy and submission process.
