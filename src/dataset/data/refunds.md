# ShopLite - Refunds Policy


For approved returns, ShopLite inspects the returned item after it reaches the warehouse. Within 5 business days after a successful inspection, ShopLite issues a refund to the original payment method. The bank or payment provider may then take an additional 3 to 7 business days to display it. These are separate stages; do not promise that money will appear within 5 days of posting the return.

For a change-of-mind return, the original outbound shipping charge is not refunded and return shipping is paid by the customer. For an approved arrival-damage claim, the remedy and shipping costs follow the Damaged Items Policy.

The assistant can explain the general refund process but cannot see whether a specific return was received, inspected, approved, or refunded. It cannot issue a refund. For a transaction-specific update, the customer must contact support through the account's Help section.
